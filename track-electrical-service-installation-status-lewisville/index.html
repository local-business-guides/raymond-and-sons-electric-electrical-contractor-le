What to ask, and how to assess the answer

A useful status request covers the work being discussed, its present stage, and what needs to happen next. The exact details depend on the installation. These key facts can help you keep the conversation focused:

  • What it covers: An update about electrical installation work, not a guarantee that the work is complete or scheduled.
  • Typical timing: No single timeline applies to every job. Ask the responsible provider what timing they can confirm and what could change it.
  • General cost factors: The requested work, the installation's requirements, and any additional work identified can affect cost. Ask what the estimate includes; no price can be assumed from a status update.
  • Provider comparison: Check that each company understands the same scope, explains the next step, and is clear about what it can confirm. Compare the answers, not just a proposed date.

You may hear a few trade terms. Scope means the work included in the job. Estimate is a stated expected cost, which may depend on the details confirmed. Status means the current stage, not a promise about when the work will finish.
