Key details, provider comparisons, and terms

The details of an installation shape the conversation. Use this list to prepare and to compare providers on the same basis:

  • What it covers: The agreed electrical work for the property. Confirm the specific tasks rather than assuming every installation request includes the same work.
  • Typical timing: Timing varies with the scope and what the job requires. Ask what can be estimated at the outset and what might affect that estimate.
  • General cost factors: The work requested, the property's needs, and additional requirements identified during review can affect the cost. Ask which items are included in an estimate and which may need separate discussion.
  • How to compare: Give each provider the same description of the job. Compare how clearly they explain the proposed scope, timing considerations, and cost factors, and ask who to contact if the details change.

Some common terms are straightforward. Scope is the set of tasks included in the work. Estimate is an expected cost based on the information available, rather than a price to assume without review. Service address is the property location where the installation is requested.
